Nondiscrimination Policy

All Inclusive Tutoring Solutions LLC · Effective 2025

Nondiscrimination Statement

All Inclusive Tutoring Solutions does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, national origin, ancestry, disability, age, marital status, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, or any other characteristic protected by applicable federal, state, or local law in the delivery of its programs, services, or activities.

All Inclusive Tutoring Solutions LLC is committed to providing equal access to high-quality educational services for every student, regardless of background or identity. We believe every student deserves the opportunity to thrive academically.

This policy applies to all aspects of our tutoring services, including but not limited to:

Enrollment & Admissions — All students are considered for enrollment without regard to any protected characteristic.

Service Delivery — All students receive the same quality of instruction, attention, and support.

Pricing & Scholarships — Rates and any available financial accommodations are applied consistently and without discrimination.

Communications — All families are communicated with respectfully and equitably.

If you believe you have experienced discrimination in connection with our services, please contact us directly:

Email: hello@allinclusivetutoring.com
Website: allinclusivetutoring.com

This policy is reviewed periodically and updated as needed to reflect changes in applicable law or organizational practice. Last updated: 2025.
