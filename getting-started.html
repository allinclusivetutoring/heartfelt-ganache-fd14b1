New to ESA? Here's Exactly How to Get Started

No jargon. No pressure. Just a clear roadmap from first question to first session.

1

Check Your State's Eligibility

  • Is your state on the approved list? (AZ, FL, LA, IN, IA, MT, NH, NC, SC, UT, WV, WY)
  • Programs have different eligibility requirements (public school enrollment, income, disability status, etc.)
  • Learn more in our full state explainer: What is ESA?
Check My State
2

Apply for Your State's ESA Program

  • Each state has its own application portal. You'll typically need: proof of state residency, student info, prior school enrollment records.
  • Processing times vary: 2–8 weeks typically.
  • Once approved, funds are loaded to ClassWallet or Odyssey quarterly.
  • Tip: Apply early — some programs have waitlists.
3

Find & Book an Approved Vendor

  • Search for "All Inclusive Tutoring Solutions LLC" in your state's approved vendor directory (ClassWallet or Odyssey).
  • We're pre-approved in all 12 states — no extra paperwork needed on your end.
  • Sessions are billed directly through the state portal — zero out-of-pocket for you.
  • Rates starting at $9.17 / 30-minute session.
4

Book Your Free 90-Minute Trial

  • Start with our introductory 90-minute trial (split as two 45-minute sessions).
  • Experience our teaching style firsthand with minimal risk.
  • If it's the right fit, we'll build a custom learning plan for your student.
