Educational Equity

Closing Opportunity Gaps

Part of my work is focused on closing opportunity gaps for students. As part of my commitment to educational reform, I build context and culture into every step of program design and evaluation using the Culturally Responsive and Equitable Evaluation (CREE) framework.

In practice, that means engaging directly with the families and communities I work with — not just consulting once to check a box, but genuinely listening throughout the process, so their voices help shape how goals are set, how progress is measured, and when an approach needs to adapt.
