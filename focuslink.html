FocusLink: Distraction-Free Tutoring Device Management

A school-grade learning-management approach to a distraction-free online learning setup — something no one else in virtual tutoring provides.

★ Exclusive to All Inclusive Tutoring Solutions LLC

FocusLink is my answer to the single biggest worry families have about online tutoring: what's actually happening on the screen when I'm not in the room. It's a school learning-management-style, distraction-free online learning setup, built to keep every session locked in on the work at hand.

A 100% guaranteed distraction-free tutoring session

During your child's tutoring session, approved educational resources remain fully available, while unrelated sites and digital distractions are temporarily restricted — on every enrolled student device — to ensure real digital focus.

How It Works

1

Enroll Your Devices

Your child's computer, iPad, iPhone, or Android device is enrolled in the seat-based device management platform ahead of your first session.

2

Session Starts, Focus Locks In

Approved educational resources stay open. Unrelated apps, games, and sites are temporarily restricted for the duration of the session.

3

Session Ends, Devices Unlock

Restrictions lift automatically the moment the session wraps up — no lasting changes to your student's device.

Seat-Based Device Management

FocusLink runs on a learning session monitoring and management platform, licensed on a seat-per-student basis. It's the same category of tool schools use to manage devices during instructional time — now built directly into your child's private tutoring sessions.
