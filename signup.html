1. Parties & Services

This Agreement is between All Inclusive Tutoring Solutions LLC ("Provider") and the individual named above ("Client"). Provider offers virtual one-on-one and small-group academic tutoring, test preparation, executive function coaching, and specialized learning support delivered by live video session.

2. Scheduling & Session Format

  • Sessions are available in 30-minute, 1-hour, 1.5-hour, and 2-hour formats, individually or as prepaid packages.
  • Small-group sessions accommodate up to four students at the same total rate; there is no per-student surcharge.
  • Extended sessions beyond two hours may be arranged for test prep, exams, capstones, and other major academic commitments.
  • Client is responsible for providing a device with a camera and a reliable internet connection.

3. Payment

  • Payment is due at time of booking unless funded through an approved Education Savings Account or scholarship platform such as ClassWallet or Odyssey.
  • Prepaid packages do not expire but are non-transferable between clients.
  • A receipt or invoice is issued to the email address provided above.

4. Cancellation Policy

Sessions cancelled or rescheduled with less than 24 hours' notice will be charged in full. Provider understands that emergencies occur and will make reasonable accommodations where possible.

Regarding the complimentary trial: if the trial package is cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, whether the trial may be rebooked will be considered on a case-by-case basis at Provider's discretion.
